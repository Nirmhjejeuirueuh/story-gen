Story: To get dry, the Dodo announced a Caucus Race. He marked out a course — a sort of circle — and everyone ran about as they pleased. When they stopped, he declared that everybody had won, and all must have prizes. Alice handed out comfits from her pocket, receiving her own thimble back as her prize.
