Story: The Mad Hatter asked Alice a riddle — why is a raven like a writing desk? — and then admitted he had absolutely no idea of the answer. He took out his pocket watch, complained it showed the wrong day, and tried dipping it in his tea to fix it. The March Hare suggested butter would have worked better.
