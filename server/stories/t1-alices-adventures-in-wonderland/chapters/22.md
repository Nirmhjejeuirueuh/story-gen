Story: The Mad Hatter was called as a witness and arrived still holding his teacup, which he was afraid to put down. The Cook was called next and brought her enormous pepper grinder. The whole court began sneezing. Through it all, Alice noticed she had been growing steadily larger — and was feeling considerably less frightened.
