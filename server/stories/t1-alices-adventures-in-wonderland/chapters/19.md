Story: The Queen sent Alice with a loud Gryphon to hear the Mock Turtle's story. They found him sitting on a lonely rock, weeping enormous slow tears. He told them about his school under the sea where the master was an old turtle — they called him Tortoise because he taught them — and the subjects included reeling and writhing.
