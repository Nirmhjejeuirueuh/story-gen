Story: Alice found a bottle on the table with a label that said DRINK ME. It tasted of cherry tart and pineapple and roast turkey — and she shrank very small indeed. Then a little cake labelled EAT ME made her shoot up so tall her head bumped the ceiling. Nothing in Wonderland, it seemed, ever stayed the right size.
