Story: On a warm golden afternoon, Alice sat by the riverbank with her sister, growing sleepy with nothing to do. Suddenly a White Rabbit ran past, pulling a watch from its waistcoat pocket and muttering "Oh dear, I shall be late!" Alice had never seen a rabbit with a waistcoat pocket before, and she jumped up at once, burning with curiosity to see where it would go.
